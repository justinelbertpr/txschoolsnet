    Where this comes from

    Ratings and scores come from data the Texas Education Agency publishes at txschools.gov, fetched 15 August 2026. Enrollment figures come from TEA's separate PEIMS Student Program reports. Both archives carry checksums so each number stays traceable to the bytes TEA served. This site is unofficial and is not affiliated with TEA.
