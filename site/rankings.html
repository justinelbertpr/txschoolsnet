    Texas school rankings

    256 ranked lists · TEA data fetched 15 August 2026

    TEA publishes a rating for every district and campus. It does not publish them in order. These tables do, and each one states the population it ranked, how many entities that was, and which were left out and why. Both ends of every ordering are published.

    How to read these

    • Every list names its population and its n. A placement without a denominator is a boast.
    • Ties are shown as ties: two districts sharing 3rd both read 3rd, and the next reads 5th.
    • Entities TEA did not rate are excluded, not counted as zero, and each page says how many.
    • Student demographics — economic disadvantage, English learners, special education — are never ranked. They describe who a school serves, not how it did.
    • 22 counties have at least 10 rated districts — the same floor every ranking on this site refuses to publish a placement under — and get a ranking board of their own. The other 231 of Texas's 253 counties fall short of that floor; each keeps its county hub page's own score-ordered list instead of a board here.

    TEA publishes most measures for the current year only. Where a list ranks change over time, the underlying figure is one TEA publishes for several years — the overall score, the domain scores, per-student spending. Nothing else here is presented as a trend.
