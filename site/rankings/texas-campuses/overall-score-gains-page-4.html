    The change in overall score since 2021-22, from TEA's re-scored "2021-22 What If" figure to the figure it published for 2025-26. TEA re-scored 2021-22 under the rules it adopted for 2022-23 and publishes that re-scoring as "2021-22 What If".     What this ranking counts

    7,283 campuses of 8,066 campuses are ranked here.

    420 were not rated by TEA for 2025-26 and are not ranked.

    363 have no figure at the start of the window and are not ranked.

    Ordered by the largest rise first. A negative figure is a decline.

    7,278 of the ranked campuses share a placement with at least one other. Tied campuses print the same placement and the next placement skips, so two campuses sharing 3rd are both 3rd and the one behind them is 5th.

    A ranking by a raw figure is not a measure of teaching quality on its own: it correlates with school size and, more strongly, with how many of a school's students live in poverty. Every entity page on this site instead compares a school against a peer group — districts with districts, campuses with campuses — whose share of economically disadvantaged students falls within 10 percentage points of its own. This list does not: it orders by the raw figure alone. How the peer group is chosen.
